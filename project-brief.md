My Project brief

Part 1: The Question

How physically accessible are Primary Health Care (PHC) facilities  to communities across Oyo West, Oyo East, Afijio, and Atiba Local Government Areas based on their spatial distribution and geographic travel thresholds?

Part 2: Why it matters? 

Accessibility to basic healthcare is necessary for developing areas like Atiba, which features a mixture of urban settlements and vast sparsely populated rural areas. In case of an emergency, the proximity to a health facility whether a local primary clinic, a private health facilities, or a general hospital can be a matter of life and death for vulnerable groups. Therefore, this project will address the siting of facilities and measure the realistic time it takes to travel to them.

Part 5: What I would build?.

I am developing a geospatial accessibility model to evaluate how easily communities within Atiba Local Government Area can physically reach healthcare services. By integrating health facility data with high-resolution population grids, road networks, and topographic data, the model computes travel-time rather than simple straight-line distances. The output is a spatial tool that visually identifies geographic healthcare "deserts" and isolates rural settlements that fall outside standard physical access thresholds, providing data-driven evidence for future health infrastructure planning.
