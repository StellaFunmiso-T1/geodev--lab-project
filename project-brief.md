My Project brief 

The Question:
How physically accessible are Primary Health Care (PHC) facilities  to communities across Oyo West, Oyo East, Afijio, and Atiba Local Government Areas based on their spatial distribution and geographic travel thresholds?

Why it matters? 
Accessibility to basic facilities like PHC is necessary for developing areas like Oyo town. In case of emergency and a need for first aid, the first call for vulnerable groups and incapable communities are PHCs. Therefore, this will answer the question of proper citing of facilities and the time it takes to travel to a facility.
